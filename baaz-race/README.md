# बाज की race — Gond-art film (1:1, 96 s)

- `baaz-race.mp4`: 1080×1080, 30 fps, with sound.
- `baaz-race-phone.mp4`: 720×720, smaller file for previewing on a phone.

There is no text and no border, and every scene fills the frame, so you can add your own voice-over and captions in the edit. The style is Gond art from Madhya Pradesh: bright pigments on black, with every animal filled with dash and dot patterns. The trees are drawn leaf by leaf.

The sound is synthesized in `audio.js`: bansuri (raag Bhupali) at the start and the end, a dholak drumroll for the 3-second race, the referee whistle, a stamp slam, scissor snips, wind, underwater sound, crickets and birds.

To rebuild: `node render.js`. To check frames: `node render.js --stills=12,40`.
